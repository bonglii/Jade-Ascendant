# Mailbox printable region lessons — 2026-10-13

1. A texture's full rectangle is not its printable region. Inspect artwork bounds and test text against inner margins; the previous suite checked only the whole paper and therefore missed text crossing ornamented edges.
2. Two columns at typical phone logical widths made the reader too narrow while pinned gifts consumed vertical space. Use one full-width reader on phones, stable Inbox/Read navigation, scroll gifts and pin only actions.
3. Native render caught the inherited header inset placing new copy behind the outer crown. Match safe insets to actual frame geometry and put close above the art; do not rely on Control-rect tests alone for opaque decoration.
4. Programmatic scrolling does not prove touch gestures reach a ScrollContainer. Ensure text/attachment descendants do not swallow drag input and prove the reader drag/release guard cannot claim an actual local reward.
5. Regression assertions can lag approved feature changes. The initial server test expected eight domains and the gift restart expected one copy. Verify the exact approved nine-domain set and the saved duplicate count; do not alter real progression or duplicate policy to make obsolete tests pass.
6. Shared LESSONS.md remains excluded from incremental patches to avoid repeated context conflicts. Ship this unique checkpoint lesson file.
