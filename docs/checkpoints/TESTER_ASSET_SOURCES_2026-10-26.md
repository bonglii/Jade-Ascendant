# Stage-thumbnail asset provenance

Inspected the 25 existing journey stage thumbnails before editing. Stage 4-3 was a blue ice texture; stage 5-3 was a top-down floor. Other stage illustrations are retained. The two replacements were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using each original target and its neighboring stage's artwork as style references. The original generated PNGs are used directly; no hand-painted modifications or third-party artwork was inserted. Portrait 1024×1536 sources import at maximum 768px for their small UI cards.

- `assets/ui/journey/stage_art/stage_4_3.png`: Frozen Lotus Basin.
- `assets/ui/journey/stage_art/stage_5_3.png`: Phoenix Ash Expanse.

Shared prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: vertical stage-selection illustration for xianxia/wuxia Android game Jade Ascendant. Image 1 is the edit target; replace the flat floor entirely. Image 2 is the style/world reference: premium painterly Chinese fantasy, not an exact composition. Portrait 2:3 full-bleed detailed environment; clear foreground, middle ground and background with dramatic depth and natural atmospheric light. Main landmark in the central 60% for a tiny card. No characters, text, UI, logos or watermark. Not a top-down texture, map, arena floor, geometric placeholder or flat-color panel.”

Frozen Lotus scene prompt: “A luminous ice-lotus altar rises from an azure glacial lake, surrounded by wuxia temples and carved bridges, icy cliffs and frozen waterfalls. Cyan-blue ice and pale snow, balanced with warm gold lamps.”

Phoenix scene prompt: “A monumental phoenix-shaped gilded shrine above an ash ravine, red-and-gold temples and broken ceremonial bridges, drifting embers and fiery ridges. Obsidian, orange, crimson and antique gold.”

No new character, weapon, boss attack frame or gameplay collision asset was changed.
