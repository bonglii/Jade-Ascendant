Request account & data deletion

You can request deletion without installing or signing into the game. Send an email to yungdevstudio@gmail.com from the Google email used for Jade Ascendant. Your public UID is helpful if known, but is not required. We verify ownership; a public UID alone is not proof.

REQUEST ACCOUNT DELETION BY EMAIL

If no email app opens, use webmail and send to yungdevstudio@gmail.com with subject Jade Ascendant - Account deletion request. This button opens a draft; it does not submit a request or delete an account until you send it.

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Never send passwords, one-time codes, card details or authentication/purchase tokens. You do not need to buy anything, reinstall the game or provide a receipt to request deletion.

What is removed

Firebase game authentication, profile/public UID, current and previous cloud snapshots, account mail/activity/announcement records and server gameplay equipment/loadouts/runs associated with your account.

What may remain

Limited purchase/wallet/refund and security evidence may be retained for fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Access is restricted and retention reviewed at least annually; the applicable reason is explained when your request is completed. These records do not constitute an active game account. Cloudflare disaster-recovery copies can retain deleted rows for up to 30 days under the service plan and are not used to reactivate deleted accounts. Shared content and other players remain intact. Account deletion does not delete your Google account or automatically request a Play refund. Local files remain on your device unless you remove them through Android app storage settings.

Minta penghapusan akun & data

Anda dapat meminta penghapusan tanpa memasang atau login ke game. Kirim email ke yungdevstudio@gmail.com dari email Google yang dipakai di Jade Ascendant. UID publik boleh disertakan jika diketahui, tetapi tidak wajib. Kami memverifikasi kepemilikan; UID publik saja bukan bukti.

MINTA PENGHAPUSAN MELALUI EMAIL

Jika aplikasi email tidak terbuka, gunakan webmail dan kirim ke yungdevstudio@gmail.com dengan subjek Jade Ascendant - Account deletion request. Tombol membuka draf; permintaan belum dikirim atau akun dihapus sampai Anda mengirim email.

Target penyelesaian adalah 30 hari setelah verifikasi. Jangan kirim password, kode sekali pakai, rincian kartu, atau token autentikasi/pembelian. Anda tidak perlu membeli, memasang ulang game, atau memiliki kuitansi untuk meminta penghapusan.

Data yang dihapus

Autentikasi game di Firebase, profil/UID publik, snapshot cloud terbaru dan sebelumnya, catatan surat/aktivitas/pengumuman akun, serta equipment/loadout/run gameplay server milik akun.

Data yang mungkin dipertahankan

Bukti pembelian/dompet/refund dan keamanan yang terbatas dapat dipertahankan untuk pencegahan penipuan, sengketa, atau kewajiban hukum. Akses dibatasi dan retensi ditinjau setidaknya setiap tahun; alasan yang berlaku dijelaskan saat permintaan selesai. Catatan ini bukan akun game aktif. Salinan pemulihan bencana Cloudflare dapat menyimpan baris yang dihapus hingga 30 hari sesuai paket layanan dan tidak dipakai untuk mengaktifkan kembali akun yang dihapus. Konten bersama dan pemain lain tetap utuh. Penghapusan tidak menghapus akun Google atau otomatis meminta refund Play. File lokal tetap di perangkat sampai Anda menghapusnya melalui pengaturan penyimpanan aplikasi Android.
