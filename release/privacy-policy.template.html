English

This policy describes the offline edition of Jade Ascendant published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}.

Game data stays on your device

The game does not transmit gameplay data, personal information, or device identifiers to the publisher or a game server. It has no player accounts, advertising SDK, third-party analytics service, social features, or real-money purchases. Progress, equipment, achievements, daily trials, checkpoints, and settings are stored locally. Daily trials and meditation use the device date. Optional vibration provides combat feedback only.

Storage and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until app storage is cleared or the game is uninstalled. On Android, open Settings > Apps > Jade Ascendant > Storage > Clear storage to remove local game data. This edition has no cloud save or account recovery. Removing local data may permanently remove progress.

Support and external links

You may contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. If you choose to send an email, the publisher receives the address and information you provide and uses them to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or signing keys. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence. Opening a policy or support link uses your external browser or email application, whose own privacy policies apply.

Children and changes

The game has no in-game form or account that requests personal information from children. Contact the publisher if personal information was sent for support and you want it removed. The publisher may update this policy when the game's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

Bahasa Indonesia

Kebijakan ini menjelaskan edisi luring Jade Ascendant yang diterbitkan oleh {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}.

Data permainan tetap di perangkat

Game tidak mengirim data permainan, informasi pribadi, atau pengenal perangkat kepada penerbit atau server game. Tidak ada akun pemain, SDK iklan, layanan analitik pihak ketiga, fitur sosial, atau pembelian dengan uang nyata. Progres, perlengkapan, pencapaian, ujian harian, checkpoint, dan pengaturan disimpan secara lokal. Ujian harian dan meditasi memakai tanggal perangkat. Getaran opsional hanya memberikan respons saat bertempur.

Penyimpanan dan penghapusan

Progres lokal tetap di perangkat sampai data aplikasi dihapus atau game dicopot. Di Android, buka Pengaturan > Aplikasi > Jade Ascendant > Penyimpanan > Hapus data untuk menghapus data lokal. Edisi ini tidak memiliki simpanan awan atau pemulihan akun. Menghapus data lokal dapat menghilangkan progres secara permanen.
