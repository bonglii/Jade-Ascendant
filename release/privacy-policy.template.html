Privacy Policy

Your game and optional backup

Progress, equipment, achievements, quests, journey checkpoints and preferences are stored on your device. If you sign in with Google and choose Save in Cloud backup, nine permanent progress domains are uploaded to our Cloudflare profile service. Automatic updates then run approximately every five minutes while safely in menus. One current snapshot and one previous snapshot are held. Active-run checkpoints and device settings are not part of that backup. Restore requires your explicit choice. Google sign-in alone does not enable progress uploads. A backup cannot recreate progress lost before its first successful save.

Google identity, public UID and mail

Guest play is available. Optional Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and may process your Google account name, email address and Firebase user identifier. Our profile service derives a private account binding and issues a separate public UID. We store that binding, public UID, server-mail delivery/read/claim records, announcement reads and recent activity/version information to operate profiles, mail and backups. Authentication tokens are used to authenticate requests; this feature does not intentionally log or archive them. Signing out does not erase device progress or server records.

Purchases and paid items

Optional Celestial Jade purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive full payment-card details. Firebase identity/App Check and our Cloudflare IAP service verify purchases, deliver paid Jade/items, prevent duplicate credits, recover interrupted delivery and reconcile refunds. The service holds account bindings, purchase verification records, token fingerprints, product/purchase state, wallet transactions, summon outcomes and paid equipment/loadouts/runs. Some verification records may include a private Firebase identifier or Play order information. Paid state is authoritative on that service and is not restored from an offline progress backup. Account deletion does not itself request a Play refund. Server-operated events also retain event progress, mission claims, reward outboxes and verified bonus entitlements needed to deliver rewards and prevent duplicate claims.

Optional rewarded ads and privacy choices

Optional rewarded ads use Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP. Depending on SDK configuration and applicable choices, Google may collect and share IP address (which can infer approximate location), ad/device or account identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads are not requested until the consent flow permits them. Where UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, it is available in Privacy & Support. Declining or failing to load an ad does not remove existing progress. Reward verification depends on the placement. Local bonuses require the matching SDK earned-reward callback and durable duplicate protection. Server-owned event Dice and Heavenly Trials bonuses also use signed AdMob server-side verification, account-bound reward intents and delivery records to prevent repeated grants. Watching an ad does not guarantee immediate delivery while verification is pending. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

Support, optional notifications and service providers

Build/version information and a small list of error source locations/kinds are kept locally for support. This feature does not automatically upload error messages, stack traces or local save payloads. If you choose to copy/send a support report or email us, we receive only what you send; review it before sharing. Firebase/Google and Cloudflare process data needed to provide authentication, app integrity, ads, purchases and our hosted services, including network/security metadata. We do not sell player data. Service-provider processing and Google advertising data practices are distinct; advertising sharing must still be disclosed in Data safety. If you allow optional Android update notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging processes an app-installation identifier/registration token and update-topic subscription to deliver release messages. The notification preference belongs to the device installation, not the signed-in Google game account. The app checks the announced version locally; you can stop notification display in Android app notification settings. Our dashboard stores announcement content and administrative sending records; it does not keep a per-player list of FCM registration tokens.

Account deletion and retention

Request deletion from Privacy & Support > Request account deletion, or from the public Account deletion page linked below, even after uninstalling. Send the request from the Google email used for the game; include your public UID only if known. We verify account ownership and aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Never send passwords, one-time codes or purchase/authentication tokens. The request is handled by support, not an instant delete button. We remove Firebase game authentication, profile/public UID, cloud snapshots and account mail/activity/announcement data, and remove server gameplay/loadouts/runs associated with the deleted account. Shared announcements and other players are unaffected. Limited purchase/wallet/refund and security evidence may be retained only as needed for fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations, with access restricted and retention reviewed at least annually. Such evidence is not an active playable account or a progress backup. We explain any applicable retention when completing a request. Deleting the game account does not delete your Google account. Local app files remain under your control: remove them through Android app storage settings if desired. Merely uninstalling does not erase server data. Cloudflare disaster-recovery backups may retain deleted rows for up to 30 days according to the service plan; these copies are not used to reactivate deleted accounts.

Security and your choices

Network services use HTTPS/TLS. Authentication and purchase services restrict account access; no storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. You may play as a guest, decline rewarded ads, avoid enabling cloud backup, sign out, request account deletion and manage applicable ad choices. The game does not request contacts, microphone, camera or precise location for these features. Contact us with privacy concerns, including a child account or an unwanted disclosure. Third-party services also provide their own privacy controls.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when features or data handling change. The effective date identifies this revision. Privacy, account deletion and support requests can be sent to the contact address displayed on this page.

Kebijakan Privasi

Game dan backup opsional

Progress, equipment, achievement, quest, checkpoint perjalanan, dan preferensi disimpan di perangkat. Jika Anda login Google lalu memilih Simpan pada Cloud backup, sembilan bagian progress permanen dikirim ke layanan profil Cloudflare kami. Setelah itu, pembaruan otomatis berjalan sekitar setiap lima menit ketika aman berada di menu. Kami menyimpan satu snapshot terbaru dan satu snapshot sebelumnya. Checkpoint run aktif serta pengaturan perangkat tidak termasuk backup. Pemulihan memerlukan pilihan Anda. Login Google saja tidak mengaktifkan upload progress. Backup tidak dapat mengembalikan progress yang hilang sebelum penyimpanan pertama berhasil.

Identitas Google, UID publik, dan surat

Mode tamu tersedia. Login Google opsional memakai Firebase Authentication dan dapat memproses nama akun Google, alamat email, serta identitas pengguna Firebase. Layanan profil membuat pengikat akun privat dan UID publik yang terpisah. Kami menyimpan pengikat tersebut, UID publik, catatan pengiriman/baca/klaim surat server, pembacaan pengumuman, serta aktivitas/versi terbaru untuk menjalankan profil, surat, dan backup. Token autentikasi digunakan untuk memverifikasi permintaan; fitur ini tidak sengaja mencatat atau mengarsipkannya. Logout tidak menghapus progress perangkat atau catatan server.

Pembelian dan item berbayar

Pembelian Celestial Jade opsional diproses Google Play Billing. Kami tidak menerima rincian lengkap kartu pembayaran. Identitas Firebase/App Check dan layanan IAP Cloudflare memverifikasi pembelian, mengirim Jade/item berbayar, mencegah kredit ganda, memulihkan pengiriman terputus, serta merekonsiliasi refund. Layanan menyimpan pengikat akun, catatan verifikasi, fingerprint token, produk/status pembelian, transaksi dompet, hasil summon, serta equipment/loadout/run berbayar. Beberapa catatan verifikasi dapat memuat identitas Firebase privat atau informasi pesanan Play. Status berbayar berasal dari layanan tersebut, bukan backup progress offline. Penghapusan akun tidak otomatis meminta refund Play. Event yang dioperasikan server juga menyimpan progres event, klaim misi, antrean hadiah, serta hak bonus terverifikasi yang diperlukan untuk mengirim hadiah dan mencegah klaim ganda.

Iklan berhadiah opsional dan pilihan privasi

Iklan berhadiah opsional memakai Google Mobile Ads dan Google UMP. Sesuai konfigurasi SDK dan pilihan yang berlaku, Google dapat mengumpulkan serta membagikan alamat IP (yang dapat memperkirakan lokasi), identitas iklan/perangkat atau akun, interaksi aplikasi/iklan, dan informasi diagnostik/performa untuk periklanan, analitik, serta pencegahan penipuan. Permintaan iklan menunggu izin dari alur persetujuan. Jika UMP memerlukan akses pilihan privasi, akses tersedia di Privacy & Support. Menolak atau gagal memuat iklan tidak menghapus progress lama. Verifikasi hadiah mengikuti penempatan iklan. Bonus lokal memerlukan callback earned-reward SDK yang sesuai dan perlindungan duplikasi tersimpan. Dadu event dan bonus Heavenly Trials yang dikelola server juga memakai verifikasi AdMob bertanda tangan dari server, intent hadiah terikat akun, serta catatan pengiriman untuk mencegah hadiah ganda. Selesai menonton tidak menjamin pengiriman langsung ketika verifikasi masih diproses. Ketersediaan iklan tidak dijamin.

Bantuan, notifikasi opsional, dan penyedia layanan

Informasi versi/build serta daftar kecil lokasi/jenis error disimpan lokal untuk bantuan. Fitur ini tidak otomatis mengunggah pesan error, stack trace, atau isi save lokal. Jika Anda memilih menyalin/mengirim laporan atau email bantuan, kami menerima informasi yang Anda kirim; periksa sebelum membagikan. Firebase/Google dan Cloudflare memproses data untuk autentikasi, integritas aplikasi, iklan, pembelian, dan layanan hosting, termasuk metadata jaringan/keamanan. Kami tidak menjual data pemain. Pemrosesan penyedia layanan berbeda dari praktik data iklan Google; pembagian untuk iklan tetap harus dinyatakan di Data safety. Jika Anda mengizinkan notifikasi update Android opsional, Firebase Cloud Messaging memproses identitas instalasi/token registrasi dan langganan topik update untuk mengirim pesan rilis. Preferensi notifikasi mengikuti instalasi perangkat, bukan akun Google game yang sedang login. Aplikasi memeriksa versi pengumuman secara lokal; tampilan notifikasi dapat dinonaktifkan melalui pengaturan notifikasi aplikasi di Android. Dashboard menyimpan isi pengumuman dan catatan pengiriman admin; dashboard tidak menyimpan daftar token registrasi FCM per pemain.

Penghapusan akun dan retensi

Minta penghapusan melalui Privacy & Support > Minta penghapusan akun, atau halaman publik Penghapusan akun di bawah, termasuk setelah aplikasi dicopot. Kirim dari email Google yang dipakai di game; sertakan UID publik hanya jika diketahui. Kami memverifikasi kepemilikan dan menargetkan penyelesaian permintaan terverifikasi dalam 30 hari. Jangan kirim password, kode sekali pakai, atau token pembelian/autentikasi. Permintaan diproses bantuan, bukan tombol hapus instan. Kami menghapus autentikasi game di Firebase, profil/UID publik, snapshot cloud, data surat/aktivitas/pengumuman akun, serta gameplay/loadout/run server milik akun tersebut. Pengumuman bersama dan pemain lain tidak terpengaruh. Bukti pembelian/dompet/refund dan keamanan yang terbatas dapat dipertahankan hanya sesuai kebutuhan pencegahan penipuan, sengketa, atau kewajiban hukum, dengan akses terbatas dan retensi ditinjau setidaknya setiap tahun. Bukti itu bukan akun aktif yang dapat dimainkan atau backup progress. Retensi yang berlaku dijelaskan ketika permintaan diselesaikan. Penghapusan akun game tidak menghapus akun Google Anda. File lokal tetap dalam kendali Anda: hapus melalui pengaturan penyimpanan aplikasi Android bila diinginkan. Mencopot aplikasi saja tidak menghapus data server. Backup pemulihan bencana Cloudflare dapat menyimpan baris yang dihapus hingga 30 hari sesuai paket layanan; salinan itu tidak digunakan untuk mengaktifkan kembali akun yang dihapus.

Keamanan dan pilihan Anda

Layanan jaringan memakai HTTPS/TLS. Autentikasi dan layanan pembelian membatasi akses per akun; tidak ada penyimpanan atau transmisi yang sepenuhnya aman. Anda dapat bermain sebagai tamu, menolak iklan berhadiah, tidak mengaktifkan backup cloud, logout, meminta penghapusan akun, serta mengelola pilihan iklan yang berlaku. Fitur ini tidak meminta kontak, mikrofon, kamera, atau lokasi presisi. Hubungi kami untuk masalah privasi, termasuk akun anak atau informasi yang tidak sengaja dibagikan. Layanan pihak ketiga juga menyediakan kontrol privasinya sendiri.

Perubahan dan kontak

Kebijakan diperbarui ketika fitur atau pengelolaan data berubah. Tanggal berlaku menunjukkan revisi ini. Pertanyaan privasi, penghapusan akun, dan bantuan dapat dikirim ke alamat kontak pada halaman ini.
