This policy describes Jade Ascendant published by {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}.

Local game data

Game progress, equipment, achievements, daily trials, run checkpoints, and preferences are stored locally on the device. Jade Ascendant currently has no player account or cloud save. Local game progress is not sent to a publisher-operated game server.

Optional rewarded advertising

On Android, Jade Ascendant uses Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy choices. According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, the SDK may automatically collect and share data such as IP address, user product interactions including ad views and taps, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. The exact ad-serving mode and available privacy choices can depend on consent, device, region, and Google configuration. Rewarded ads are optional; declining or not completing one does not remove gameplay progress, and an in-game reward is granted only after the ad SDK confirms completion.

For information about how Google uses data when you use apps that use its services, see Google's partner sites and apps information.

Purchases

This version does not currently offer a real-money purchase flow. If billing is added in a later release, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

Storage and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until app storage is cleared or the game is uninstalled. On Android, open Settings > Apps > Jade Ascendant > Storage > Clear storage to remove local game data. There is currently no account recovery for deleted local progress. Advertising and consent data handled by Google is governed separately by Google's services and the privacy choices available to the user.

Support and external links

You may contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. If you choose to send an email, the publisher receives the address and information you provide and uses them to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or signing keys. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence. Opening a policy or support link uses your external browser or email application, whose own privacy policies apply.

Children and changes

The game does not provide a player account or an in-game form for entering personal information. Advertising treatment, consent, and store disclosures are configured according to the app's distribution settings and applicable requirements. The publisher may update this policy when the game's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.

Bahasa Indonesia

Kebijakan ini menjelaskan Jade Ascendant yang diterbitkan oleh {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}.

Data game lokal

Progres game, perlengkapan, pencapaian, ujian harian, checkpoint sesi, dan preferensi disimpan secara lokal di perangkat. Jade Ascendant saat ini tidak memiliki akun pemain atau cloud save. Progres game lokal tidak dikirim ke server game yang dioperasikan penerbit.

Iklan berhadiah opsional

Di Android, Jade Ascendant menggunakan Google Mobile Ads untuk menyediakan iklan berhadiah opsional dan Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) untuk mengelola pilihan privasi yang berlaku. Menurut pengungkapan Google Mobile Ads SDK, SDK dapat secara otomatis mengumpulkan dan membagikan data seperti alamat IP, interaksi pengguna termasuk tampilan dan ketukan iklan, informasi diagnostik, serta ID perangkat atau akun untuk tujuan periklanan, analisis, dan pencegahan penipuan. Mode penayangan iklan dan pilihan privasi yang tersedia dapat bergantung pada persetujuan, perangkat, wilayah, serta konfigurasi Google. Iklan berhadiah bersifat opsional; menolak atau tidak menyelesaikan iklan tidak mengurangi progres permainan, dan hadiah dalam game hanya diberikan setelah SDK iklan mengonfirmasi penyelesaian.

Untuk informasi tentang cara Google menggunakan data saat Anda memakai aplikasi yang menggunakan layanannya, lihat informasi situs dan aplikasi partner Google.

Pembelian

Versi ini saat ini belum menyediakan alur pembelian dengan uang nyata. Jika billing ditambahkan pada rilis berikutnya, kebijakan ini dan pengungkapan toko yang relevan akan diperbarui sebelum fitur tersebut dirilis.

Penyimpanan dan penghapusan

Progres lokal tetap berada di perangkat sampai data aplikasi dihapus atau game dicopot. Di Android, buka Pengaturan > Aplikasi > Jade Ascendant > Penyimpanan > Hapus data untuk menghapus data game lokal. Saat ini tidak ada pemulihan akun untuk progres lokal yang telah dihapus. Data iklan dan persetujuan yang ditangani Google diatur secara terpisah oleh layanan Google dan pilihan privasi yang tersedia bagi pengguna.
