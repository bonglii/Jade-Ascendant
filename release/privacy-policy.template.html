This Privacy Policy explains how {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} handles information in connection with Jade Ascendant.

1. Game data stored on your device

Jade Ascendant stores gameplay information such as progress, equipment, achievements, daily-quest state, active-run checkpoints, settings, currency balances, Pavilion state, and purchase-delivery records in the app's local storage. Gameplay progress remains local to the device. Production Cloud Save is not currently available.

2. Optional Google Sign-In

On supported Android builds, you may continue as Guest or optionally sign in with Google through Firebase Authentication. Google Sign-In establishes player identity; it does not upload, download, merge, switch, back up, or restore local gameplay progress.

The current game code keeps the authenticated Firebase UID as a private identity boundary. It is not exposed in the account UI, written to the gameplay save, or intentionally logged by Jade Ascendant. The account UI may display the account name returned by the authentication provider. Signing out does not delete local gameplay progress.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

On Android, Jade Ascendant uses Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy choices.

According to Google's disclosures for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK may automatically collect and share information including IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Google states that these data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and that data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

Rewarded ads are optional. Declining an ad, closing it before the reward is confirmed, or being unable to load an ad does not remove existing gameplay progress. An in-game reward is granted only after the advertising SDK confirms completion.

4. Consent and ad privacy choices

Where required, Google UMP may display a consent or privacy message before ads are requested. Available options depend on the user's region, device, Google configuration, and applicable requirements. When Google indicates that privacy options are required, Jade Ascendant provides access to those options from the in-game Privacy & Support screen.

5. Google Play purchases

Jade Ascendant may offer optional one-time in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. Jade Ascendant does not receive or store your full payment-card details.

The Google Play Billing service may provide the app with purchase information such as product ID, purchase state, purchase token, acknowledgement/consumption state, and order information. Jade Ascendant uses this information for local delivery of purchased in-game content, recovery of eligible interrupted purchases, and duplicate-grant protection. Purchase-delivery records used by the current client are stored in the game's local save.

A purchase in a pending state does not grant in-game rewards until Google Play reports it as purchased. Consumable products are finalized only after the corresponding local reward/save operation succeeds. Production server-side purchase-token verification is not currently integrated.

6. Third-party services

Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, advertising, consent, and Play purchase functionality are provided through Google/Firebase services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. More information is available at:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

7. Support communications

If you contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use this information to respond to support, privacy, or other requests. Support correspondence may also be processed by our email service provider.

8. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay and purchase-delivery data can be removed by clearing Jade Ascendant's app storage or uninstalling the app, subject to Android or Google backup/restore behavior. Google Sign-In is not gameplay backup, so signing in does not let {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} restore deleted local gameplay progress.

Information processed by Google/Firebase for authentication, advertising, consent, and Play purchases is handled under those services and applicable account/privacy settings. This policy does not claim a separate retention period for data controlled by those services.

9. Security

Guest mode remains available and Jade Ascendant does not ask you to create a game password. Google authentication credentials/tokens are handled through the native Google/Firebase integration rather than stored in the gameplay save. Payment credentials are handled by Google Play rather than by Jade Ascendant. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

Google Sign-In is optional and Guest mode is available. Jade Ascendant does not include an in-game form asking players to submit personal information directly to {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}. Authentication, advertising, consent, and Play purchase data may still be handled by Google/Firebase according to the app's distribution settings, privacy choices, account settings, and applicable requirements. A parent or guardian with a privacy question may contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Jade Ascendant's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

Bahasa Indonesia

Kebijakan Privasi ini menjelaskan cara {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} menangani informasi sehubungan dengan Jade Ascendant.

1. Data game yang disimpan di perangkat

Jade Ascendant menyimpan informasi permainan seperti progres, equipment, achievement, status daily quest, checkpoint sesi aktif, pengaturan, saldo mata uang, status Pavilion, dan catatan pengiriman pembelian di penyimpanan lokal aplikasi. Progres gameplay tetap tersimpan lokal di perangkat. Production Cloud Save saat ini belum tersedia.

2. Google Sign-In opsional

Pada build Android yang mendukung, Anda dapat melanjutkan sebagai Guest atau login secara opsional dengan Google melalui Firebase Authentication. Google Sign-In membentuk identitas pemain; fitur ini tidak mengunggah, mengunduh, menggabungkan, mengganti, mencadangkan, atau memulihkan progres gameplay lokal.

Code game saat ini memperlakukan Firebase UID yang terautentikasi sebagai batas identitas privat. UID tidak ditampilkan di UI akun, tidak ditulis ke gameplay save, dan tidak sengaja dicatat ke log oleh Jade Ascendant. UI akun dapat menampilkan nama akun yang diberikan provider autentikasi. Sign out tidak menghapus progres gameplay lokal.

3. Iklan berhadiah opsional

Di Android, Jade Ascendant menggunakan Google Mobile Ads untuk menyediakan iklan berhadiah opsional dan Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) untuk mengelola pilihan privasi yang berlaku.

Menurut pengungkapan Google untuk Google Mobile Ads SDK, SDK dapat secara otomatis mengumpulkan dan membagikan informasi termasuk alamat IP, interaksi pengguna dengan produk, informasi diagnostik, serta ID perangkat atau akun. Google menyatakan bahwa data tersebut dapat digunakan untuk periklanan, analisis, dan pencegahan penipuan, serta data yang dikumpulkan SDK dienkripsi selama transit.

Iklan berhadiah bersifat opsional. Menolak iklan, menutupnya sebelum hadiah dikonfirmasi, atau tidak dapat memuat iklan tidak menghapus progres permainan yang sudah ada. Hadiah dalam game hanya diberikan setelah SDK iklan mengonfirmasi penyelesaian.

4. Persetujuan dan pilihan privasi iklan

Jika diwajibkan, Google UMP dapat menampilkan pesan persetujuan atau privasi sebelum iklan diminta. Pilihan yang tersedia bergantung pada wilayah pengguna, perangkat, konfigurasi Google, dan persyaratan yang berlaku. Saat Google menyatakan opsi privasi diperlukan, Jade Ascendant menyediakan akses ke opsi tersebut melalui layar Privacy & Support di dalam game.

5. Pembelian Google Play

Jade Ascendant dapat menawarkan pembelian dalam aplikasi satu kali yang bersifat opsional melalui Google Play Billing. Pembayaran diproses oleh Google Play. Jade Ascendant tidak menerima atau menyimpan detail lengkap kartu pembayaran Anda.

Layanan Google Play Billing dapat memberikan informasi pembelian kepada aplikasi seperti ID produk, status pembelian, purchase token, status acknowledgement/consumption, dan informasi pesanan. Jade Ascendant menggunakan informasi tersebut untuk pengiriman lokal konten dalam game yang dibeli, pemulihan pembelian terputus yang memenuhi syarat, serta perlindungan dari pemberian hadiah ganda. Catatan pengiriman pembelian yang digunakan client saat ini disimpan di save lokal game.

Pembelian berstatus pending tidak memberikan hadiah dalam game sampai Google Play melaporkannya sebagai purchased. Produk consumable baru difinalisasi setelah pemberian reward dan penyimpanan lokal terkait berhasil. Verifikasi purchase token secara server-side untuk produksi saat ini belum terintegrasi.

6. Layanan pihak ketiga

Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, fungsi iklan, persetujuan, dan pembelian Play disediakan melalui layanan Google/Firebase serta tunduk pada ketentuan dan praktik privasi masing-masing layanan. Informasi lebih lanjut:

  • Cara Google menggunakan informasi dari situs atau aplikasi yang menggunakan layanannya
  • Kebijakan Privasi Google

Data gameplay lokal dan catatan pengiriman pembelian dapat dihapus dengan menghapus penyimpanan aplikasi Jade Ascendant atau mencopot aplikasi, dengan tetap memperhatikan perilaku backup/restore Android atau Google. Google Sign-In bukan backup gameplay, sehingga login Google tidak membuat {{PUBLISHER_NAME}} dapat memulihkan progres lokal yang sudah terhapus.
