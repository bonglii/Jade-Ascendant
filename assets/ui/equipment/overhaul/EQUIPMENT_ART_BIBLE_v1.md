# Jade Ascendant — Equipment Asset Art Bible v1

## Global
- Transparent asset only. UI owns rarity frames/backgrounds.
- Master canvas: 512×512.
- Keep ~15–20% clear padding.
- Readable silhouette at 64–96 px.
- Top-left key light, controlled cyan spiritual rim.
- No text, characters, numbers, hands, bodies, scenery, UI frames, or external cast shadows.
- Painterly/full-color material rendering, not monochrome line glyphs.

## Rarity language
- Common: practical jade/bronze/linen; minimal aura.
- Rare: cyan steel, silver, refined jade; controlled mist/Qi.
- Epic: stronger unique silhouette, violet/crimson/celestial accents, signature aura.
- Legendary: warm gold + cyan core, prestige geometry/halo, still clean at small scale.

## Slot cameras
- Armament: full object, lower-left → upper-right diagonal.
- Robe: 3/4 front floating garment; shoulders and hem readable.
- Bracer: isolated forearm guard at 3/4 angle; never include a human hand.
- Boots: paired footwear in shallow V, 3/4 view.
- Pendant: centered frontal hanging object with complete chain/cord silhouette.

## Production rule
Do not generate the remaining 20 items until Armament 5/5 has been runtime-validated in:
1. Hero equipped slot.
2. Arsenal grid.
3. Equipment detail / inspect.
4. Backpack item card.
